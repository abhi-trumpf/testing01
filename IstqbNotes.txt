💼 In Project Management
Purpose is the "Why": It is the ultimate goal, the reason the project exists, or the problem you are trying to solve.

Scope is the "What" and "How much": It sets the exact boundaries. It defines the specific features, tasks, deliverables, and data that will be included in the project—and just as importantly, what is excluded.
